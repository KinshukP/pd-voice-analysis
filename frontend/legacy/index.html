Steady “ahhh” at constant pitch and volume for the whole 5 seconds, in a quiet room. At least 5 takes; 7 recommended, so a bad one can be dropped and still leave enough. Use the same microphone, distance, and room for every take — switching setups partway through makes the recordings harder to compare.
